Put it on your calendar in one line.

Day is a Mac menu-bar app for the moment you need to schedule something while you are busy doing something else. Type a sentence, check exactly what it will create, press Return. The event lands on the calendar you already use.

Example

Call Sarah tomorrow at 3pm for 45 minutes

Mac App Store release is being prepared; TestFlight testers are invited by the owner. No store link yet.

No time in the line? Day asks “When?”

If your line has no time, Day asks When? after you press Return. It keeps your text and saves nothing until you add a time and press Return.

Optional Markdown notes

Want notes too? Choose a folder in Settings and Day keeps one plain file per day — not required.

Works with your calendar

Day works with the calendars already on your Mac, with your permission. It asks for Calendar access when you save your first event, not at launch, and you can choose which calendar receives new events in Settings.

Menu bar and shortcut

Day lives in the menu bar. Press ⌃⌥Space (or your own shortcut) in any app to open the panel; the main window stays closed. Esc closes the panel without saving.

Widgets

Widgets show what is next on your calendar; checking off a task needs a notes folder.

Privacy first

No account, no analytics, no third-party SDK. Day reaches your calendar through macOS Calendar only after you grant permission, and it never uploads calendar data. If your notes folder is in a sync service, that provider syncs it. Read the Day privacy policy.
