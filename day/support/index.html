Day Support

Day is a Mac app for daily Markdown notes, tasks, and calendar planning, developed by Pavel Zarudnev. Start here if Day cannot find your notes or calendar.

Day requires macOS 14 or later. Email pzarudnev93@gmail.com if you still need help.

Find or recover your notes folder

Day saves your notes in the folder you chose, in files named YYYY-MM-DD.md. You can open them with any text editor.

  1. In Day, open Day → Settings… → Notes.
  2. Click Reveal in Finder to see the folder and today's file.
  3. If Day cannot open the folder, reconnect any external drive, then click Change… and choose the folder again. If the folder was moved or renamed, choose its new location.

If the folder is missing

Restore it from your backup or sync service first. Do not create a new empty folder with the same name and assume your notes are there; Day will show only the files that exist in the folder you select.

Day remembers access to the folder with a security-scoped bookmark. If the folder is on a synced service, wait for that service to finish syncing before you reselect it. Keep a backup of the folder.

Calendar access and writable calendars

Calendar access is optional. Notes and tasks work without it.

Events do not appear

  1. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Calendars and enable Day.
  2. Quit and reopen Day.
  3. Check that the calendar account is set up in macOS Calendar or Internet Accounts. Day shows the calendars macOS Calendar already syncs; it does not add accounts itself.

You cannot schedule an event

  1. In Day, open Day → Settings… → Calendar.
  2. Under Calendar for new events, choose a calendar that allows changes.
  3. Read-only calendars, such as some subscribed calendars, cannot receive new events. If the list has no writable calendar, add one in Calendar or Internet Accounts, then reopen Day.

You can revoke Calendar access at any time in System Settings. Day keeps working for notes and tasks.

Report an issue

Email pzarudnev93@gmail.com with:

  • The Day version and build from Day → About Day.
  • Your macOS version.
  • What you did, what you expected, and what happened.
  • Whether Calendar access is on, and whether the notes folder is local or synced.

Do not send private attachments

Send only what you are comfortable sharing. Do not attach your notes folder, a calendar export, or screenshots containing private notes, events, email addresses, or other personal data. If a screenshot is needed, use a disposable notes folder and a test calendar, or redact the private parts.
